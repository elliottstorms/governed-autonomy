# Operator manual starter

Copy this into `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` (personal, applies everywhere) or a project's `CLAUDE.md` (applies in that folder), then replace every bracketed placeholder with your own words. Claude Code reads this file at the start of every session, so what you write here is standing policy, not a suggestion.

Two rules for writing it well: state hard lines as hard lines with no exception path, and give the reason next to any rule that could be misapplied without it. Delete any section you are not ready to write honestly; a placeholder rule you do not mean is worse than no rule.

---

```markdown
# CLAUDE.md - [Your name]'s operator manual

> NORTH STAR: [one sentence on what all this work is actually for]

## Tier 1 - Safety and irreversibility

- Never move or spend money: no purchases, no transactions, no deploys
  that bill. Stage everything; I run the final commands.
- Never enter credentials, tokens, or payment details anywhere.
- Messages to real people: draft and stage, never send without my go.
- Nothing goes live on a public surface under my name without my
  explicit go.
- Archive before you delete: anything removed gets its original text
  saved to an archive file first.
- [add your own: what would genuinely hurt if it happened once?]

## Tier 2 - Hard constraints

- [rules that override convenience, e.g. "never auto-switch models",
  "X and Y projects never share copy", "never assert something does
  not exist until you have looked this session"]

## Tier 3 - Workflow defaults

- Deliverables go where I act on them: [e.g. drafts into my email
  drafts folder, documents into ~/Documents/projects/...], and hand
  back the full path.
- Write absolute dates in any file that outlives today ("2026-09-04",
  never "tomorrow").
- Lead with the answer; one question per message, with your
  recommended default.
- [how you want long work reported, when to check in, etc.]

## Tier 4 - Style and voice

- [how you write: sentence length, formatting you hate, words you ban,
  how humor should or should not show up]

## Tier 5 - Who I am and what is running

- [your role, your projects, what each one is, what matters most]
- [current priorities in rank order]
```

---

Start small. Five honest rules in Tier 1 on day one beat fifty aspirational ones, and the manual grows every time something goes wrong: that is the system working, not the system failing.
